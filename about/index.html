Why I work this way

Real estate decisions are rarely about one number. Price, financing, carrying cost, property condition, commute, neighbourhood fit, future resale and negotiation risk all interact. My approach is to make those trade-offs visible before a client commits.

My background in technology leadership and architecture reinforces that discipline: define the objective, test assumptions, compare options, identify risk, and then execute with a clear plan. Real estate still requires judgement and human context, but better decisions usually start with better information.
