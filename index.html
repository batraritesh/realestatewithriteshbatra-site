My specialty

Decision-first real estate, not sales pressure.

My specialty is structured real estate decision support: compare the numbers, surface the trade-offs and understand the risks before making a commitment. That means looking beyond list price to carrying cost, neighbourhood fit, property condition, resale implications, offer strategy and the alternatives you are giving up.

Analyze the numbers

Price, financing, taxes, fees, maintenance, rent potential and total carrying cost are considered together—not in isolation.

Make trade-offs visible

I explain the pros, cons and risks so you can compare properties, neighbourhoods and timing choices without being rushed into a transaction.

Execute with a clear plan

Once the decision makes sense, negotiation, conditions, paperwork and closing steps are handled with structure and attention to detail.

Strategy before transaction.

Pre-construction & new builds

Pre-Construction & New-Build Expertise

I help buyers evaluate builder opportunities from the first project conversation through final closing — with practical experience across communities in Oakville, Whitby, Kitchener, Cambridge and surrounding markets.

Builder relationships & project guidance

Experience working with builder sales teams and projects involving Mattamy Homes, Caivan and Activa, with a focus on getting clients informed early about communities, releases and available opportunities.

Project, lot & floorplan strategy

Compare builder reputation, community plans, lots, layouts, incentives, deposit schedules, upgrade decisions, assignment terms and resale considerations before committing.

Contract-to-closing guidance

Support through registration, worksheet strategy, APS review coordination, deposits, upgrades, occupancy or interim steps where applicable, and preparation for final closing.

Recent experience includes: Mattamy Ashburn in Whitby, Mattamy pre-construction in Kitchener, Activa communities in Kitchener/Cambridge, and Caivan opportunities in Oakville.
