For sellers

Price, position and negotiate with evidence.

A strong sale plan is built around how buyers will compare your home with the alternatives available at the same time.

1. Diagnose the competition

Recent sales matter, but so do active listings, terminated listings, days on market, price reductions and the condition of competing homes. Your buyer sees the same alternatives.

2. Prepare what changes buyer perception

Decluttering, cleaning, lighting, minor repairs, photography and presentation can matter more than expensive renovations. The objective is to remove friction, not overspend before listing.

3. Treat list price as strategy

List price influences which buyers see the home, what they compare it against and how much urgency is created. It should be chosen deliberately rather than as an emotional statement of value.

4. Evaluate the whole offer

Price is only one component. Conditions, deposit, financing risk, closing date and the buyer’s ability to perform can materially affect the quality of an offer.

5. Keep the transaction moving after acceptance

Conditions, lawyer coordination, buyer visits, documents and closing logistics still require attention after the agreement is signed.
